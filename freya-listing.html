About This Opportunity

Freya is a Spokane women's clothing boutique built around a simple, bold idea: wear what feels like you. Their tagline — "Shameless Self Love" — says everything about who walks through the door. These are women shopping with intention, not obligation, choosing clothes that actually reflect who they are.

Beyond clothing, Freya carries accessories including their signature "Adapt," "Balance," and other charm hair tie bracelets — small, giftable items that bring in a broader range of shoppers and give regulars a reason to come back even when they're not in the market for a full outfit.

With an online store available worldwide and a physical boutique open Tuesday through Saturday, Freya reaches both local Spokane shoppers who browse in-store and an engaged digital following. Advertising here puts your brand alongside a style-forward, self-affirming women's community.

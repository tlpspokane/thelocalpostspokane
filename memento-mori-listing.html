About This Opportunity

Memento Mori Industries is a curated vintage shop tucked upstairs from Entropy Records in the Parkade — one of downtown Spokane's most beloved and culturally rich buildings. The store carries an eclectic mix of vintage home décor, furniture, original art, barware, vinyl records, music memorabilia, and clothing, drawing in shoppers who are intentional, curious, and creatively driven.

This isn't a mass-market retail environment — it's a destination. Customers come specifically to browse, discover, and spend time. That unhurried, exploratory shopping experience makes this an unusually receptive audience for advertising. A flyer, card, or product sample encountered here carries far more weight than one slipped into a generic shopping bag.

The Parkade location also places Memento Mori squarely in the flow of downtown Spokane's music and arts scene — neighboring Entropy Records means crossover traffic from vinyl collectors, music lovers, and cultural creatives who are some of the most engaged local shoppers in the city.

About This Opportunity

Diane Harder is a Spokane-area artist who teaches private and small-group acrylic painting classes from her home studio. She keeps classes intentionally small — typically 1 or 2 students, with larger groups of up to 4 every other month — so every student gets personal attention from a working artist.

Classes cover a wide range of subjects: eagles, jellyfish, floral scenes, wine still lifes, Mt. Fuji, cats, and more. All ages and skill levels are welcome. Sundays are popular but scheduling is flexible. Diane also sells her original paintings and is displayed at Second Look Books in the area.

Beyond her classes, Diane leads the Facebook group "Painting with the Mod Squad" — a private community of 6,100+ artists and art lovers she actively keeps alive with daily posts, tutorials, and encouragement. That's a meaningful online platform that reaches far beyond each class session.

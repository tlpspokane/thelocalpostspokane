About This Opportunity

Chicana Esthetics is one of Spokane's most distinctive and mission-driven beauty businesses — the only esthetics practice in the area specifically designed to treat multicultural skin. Founded by Latina master esthetician Abigail Palacios, who trained under Michelle Obama's personal esthetician, the practice serves clients of color dealing with hyperpigmentation, hypopigmentation, acne, scarring, and skin conditions that standard esthetic training often overlooks.

Clients don't just come in for a facial — they come in for a relationship. Programs run 3–6 months, with clients returning consistently to track real skin progress. That means Abigail's clients are loyal, engaged, and deeply trusting of the brands and businesses she chooses to associate with. Team member Jamilah Al-Baloushi, a Colville Tribal member and licensed esthetician, further extends the practice's commitment to serving diverse communities.

With a growing presence on TikTok and Instagram through Korean skincare, scalp treatment, and multicultural skin content that resonates far beyond Spokane, advertising here offers access to both a deeply local loyal clientele and an engaged digital following in a niche that's having a major cultural moment.

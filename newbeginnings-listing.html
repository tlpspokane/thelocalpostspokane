About This Opportunity

NewBeginnings Student Loan Advocacy is Spokane's premier student loan advocacy firm — and the only organization in the country focused exclusively on individualized student loan repayment and forgiveness strategy. Founded by Carey Donaldson over 10 years ago, the firm has helped clients navigate the most complex student loan landscapes imaginable, securing more than $85 million in loan forgiveness for borrowers across the country.

Carey and her team — COO Riley Hildebrand and Client Relations Manager Olivia McKinney — serve educators, administrators, therapists, parents, new graduates, and working professionals navigating federal loan systems that are in constant flux. Their Individualized Student Loan Assessment (ISLA) process creates a fully personalized repayment and forgiveness roadmap for each client, and their advocacy continues for the entire life of the loan journey.

NewBeginnings hosts regular public workshops, Q&A events, and community outreach sessions throughout Spokane — creating recurring, engaged audiences of borrowers in active financial planning mode. Advertising here means your brand is associated with financial empowerment, hope, and fresh starts.

🎓 What NewBeginnings Does

📋
Individualized Student Loan Assessment (ISLA)
A thorough, personalized two-part assessment — Part One shows your current reality, Part Two maps your path forward. $429 · Initial call always free (45 min).
🏛️
Public Service Loan Forgiveness (PSLF)
Guidance for teachers, principals, therapists, nonprofit workers, and government employees navigating the PSLF program from start to forgiveness
📉
Strategic Repayment Planning
Income-driven repayment, consolidation, SAVE alternatives, and minimization strategies tailored to each borrower's career and income
🚨
Default Management
Helping borrowers in default navigate rehabilitation options to prevent wage garnishment and tax refund interception
👨‍👩‍👧
Parent PLUS Loan Guidance
Strategy for parents navigating borrowing for their children's education — repayment options and forgiveness eligibility
🎓
College Financial Planning
FAFSA guidance, pre-borrowing literacy for families, and strategic loan planning before students take out their first loan

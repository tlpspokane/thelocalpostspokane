About This Opportunity

The U-District Foundation Mentoring Program is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit based in Spokane that pairs committed adult mentors with vulnerable youth for regular outings in the community. Mentors meet their mentees twice a month for two-plus hour outings — bowling, ice skating, volunteering at Second Harvest, visiting bookstores, playing laser tag, and more.

This listing is different from a traditional advertising placement. Sponsors here aren't just buying visibility — they're putting their business name behind a mission that Spokane families and community members genuinely care about. When your brand shows up at a mentoring outing or in the program's communications, it lands with warmth and meaning.

The program sends mentors a weekly newsletter with outing suggestions, upcoming events, free/discounted passes to local venues, and community connections. Sponsors featured in that newsletter reach a motivated, community-minded group of Spokane adults.

⚠️ Please Consider Before Booking

Because this program serves vulnerable youth and their families, U-District Mentoring is selective about sponsor alignment. Businesses involving alcohol, adult entertainment, or anything inconsistent with a youth-serving nonprofit will not be accepted. When in doubt, reach out to confirm fit before submitting a request.

💚 The Impact of This Program

  • 📉Youth in the program are less likely to start using drugs and alcohol
  • 🎓Students set higher educational goals and are more likely to attend college
  • 🏫They are less likely to skip school or classes
  • ⚽More likely to engage in athletics and extracurricular activities
  • 😊Experience fewer symptoms of depression

Sponsoring this program means your business is directly associated with these outcomes — and with the mentors and families who make them happen.

Meet Kayla — Spokane's Local Adventure Guide

Kayla is the founder and creator behind Adventures in Spokane — a family-focused blog and Instagram account dedicated to helping people fall in love with Spokane. From kid-friendly hikes to the best happy hours in Spokane Valley, to road trips across the Inland Northwest, Kayla documents it all with high-quality photography and detailed, genuinely useful guides. She's also a mom, which means her content is deeply practical and trusted by families who actually want to go do things together.

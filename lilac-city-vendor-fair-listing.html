About This Opportunity

Lilac City Vendor Fair is Spokane's newest summer outdoor market — a free, family-friendly event running every Friday, Saturday, and Sunday from May through August in the Northtown Mall parking lot. Founded by Rebeccah Benedict (also founder of Little Local Artist), the fair was built on a simple idea: vendor events should be run by people who understand what vendors actually need.

With booth rentals at just $50/day (and $30/day for community helpers), the fair deliberately keeps barriers low for small businesses and emerging artists. The result is a diverse, energetic rotating lineup of 40+ vendors — artists, crafters, coffee shops, skincare brands, food vendors, and more — that changes daily, so every visit is a new discovery.

Beyond the vendors themselves, the fair features free and low-cost workshops, live artists, and giant lawn and board games — creating a true community gathering space, not just a shopping event. Advertisers here are reaching a Spokane audience that has already chosen to spend their weekend outdoors at a local market. They're in discovery mode, in a good mood, and surrounded by the kind of community-rooted businesses The Local Post is built for.

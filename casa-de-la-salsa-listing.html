About This Opportunity

Casa de la Salsa is Spokane's premier Latin dance social club, hosting recurring Salsa, Bachata, Merengue, and Cumbia events that draw a vibrant, engaged crowd of young professionals, couples, and community members.

These aren't passive audiences — attendees arrive excited, stay for 2–4 hours, and are fully present all evening. Your brand shows up in an environment of energy, celebration, and community trust. Sponsors are recognized as supporters of something people genuinely love and return to month after month.

Beyond regular indoor socials at venues like Satori Studio, Casa de la Salsa now collaborates with River Street Latin Group on "Salsa in the Park" — a free outdoor summer series at Mirabeau Park that brings the same energy to a wider community audience across four dates this summer.

👥 Who You're Reaching

Young Professionals Adults & Couples Ages 21–45 Spokane Locals Latin Dance Community All Ages Welcome Diverse Community Outdoor Event Goers

This audience is highly social and community-oriented — they share experiences on social media, support local businesses, and return event after event. Sponsoring here means repeated exposure to the same engaged, loyal crowd over time, now across both indoor venues and the new outdoor park series.

  • 📋Name or logo displayed on a sponsor slide (indoor events) or sponsor signage (Salsa in the Park)
  • 🤝Recognition as a community supporter at the event

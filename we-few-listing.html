We F.E.W. We Happy F.E.W.

We F.E.W. (Filmmakers of Eastern Washington) is a Spokane-based nonprofit providing community engagement and workforce development for members of the Eastern Washington film scene. Running monthly mixers, classes, and full-scale film productions, they work to keep community confidence high by ensuring everyone — at any experience level — has access to professional-level facilities, equipment, hands-on training, and direct connections to industry professionals.

🎬

The Apple Box — Community Creative Space

We F.E.W. manages The Apple Box, a community access arts and media space perched above the historic Garland Theater in Spokane's Garland District. Managed by community volunteers, The Apple Box gives local filmmakers and creatives a dedicated physical home — a real space above one of Spokane's most beloved and storied cultural landmarks, where films like Benny & Joon were shot in the neighborhood.

About This Opportunity

We F.E.W. has grown to 2,400+ members in under two years — a remarkable pace that reflects how deeply Spokane's creative community was waiting for this kind of organization. The Facebook group alone serves as the primary hub where members post about projects, share opportunities, ask questions, and connect across every filmmaking department: directors, cinematographers, writers, actors, editors, sound designers, and passionate newcomers.

Monthly mixers are the heartbeat of the community — real in-person gatherings where people show up because they care. The Apple Box above the Garland Theater gives members a physical creative home that's become a destination. And productions give sponsors something rare: a credit in an actual film, made by and for the Eastern Washington creative community.

We F.E.W. has also hosted large-scale events at the Garland Theater itself — including a free Oscar Night screening open to the public, complete with ballots, dress code, and a red carpet. These events draw from well beyond the core membership and put sponsors in front of Spokane's broader arts and culture audience.

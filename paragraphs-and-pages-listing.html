About This Opportunity

Paragraphs & Pages is Liberty Lake's brand new independent bookstore — opened June 20, 2026 by co-owners Nathan and Stephanie Hansen. Nathan brings nine years of experience at the Community Library Network; Stephanie has loved books her whole life and set out to create a welcoming space for people to explore stories and connect with other readers.

This isn't a transactional retail environment. The store features a cozy seating area in the back surrounded by books and a faux fireplace — designed to invite people to linger, browse, and return. Alongside a wide selection of books, they carry sideline items like candles, stickers, bookmarks, and bags.

What makes this placement especially valuable is the programming. Paragraphs & Pages hosts two recurring book clubs per month and has author visits and signings already booked through October. That recurring calendar creates a loyal, repeat audience of readers who come back to the store again and again — exactly the kind of community that makes local advertising stick.

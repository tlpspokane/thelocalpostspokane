About This Opportunity

La Doña Cafecito is South Hill's elevated Mexican dining destination — serving breakfast, lunch, and dinner seven days a week at 1227 S Grand Blvd. With a 4.5-star Google rating, a menu that spans traditional Mexican classics and bold modern dishes, and a stunning dining room with terracotta floors, gorgeous booths, and a water feature, La Doña has quickly established itself as one of Spokane's most beloved restaurants.

What makes this placement strong is the full-day operation. Breakfast brings the neighborhood crowd starting at 8am. Lunch brings the South Hill workday set. Dinner — with craft cocktails and margaritas flowing — brings couples, groups, and families celebrating. La Doña also caters events and provides taco bars, making it a restaurant that shows up in people's most memorable moments well beyond the dining room.

Advertising here means your brand is seen by a loyal, neighborhood-rooted audience across breakfast, lunch, and dinner — and by a restaurant that genuinely takes pride in hospitality. As the reviews put it: "the most authentic and outstanding Mexican restaurant in the entire Spokane region."

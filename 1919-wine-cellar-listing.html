About This Opportunity

1919 Wine Cellar is a wine bar and wine tasting destination in Spokane's Union District — one of the city's most active and growing neighborhoods. Open seven days a week with a different event or promotion every single night, the bar has built a loyal local following of adults who come back week after week for live music, Song Bingo, karaoke, and Spokane's best mid-week wine deal.

What makes this placement unusually strong is the combination of frequency and mood. Customers at 1919 are there to enjoy themselves — they're relaxed, social, and in a celebratory frame of mind. That's a very different advertising moment than a grocery store or waiting room. A well-placed card, a sponsored event night, or a social mention from a venue they love lands with genuine warmth.

The Pinot's Palette crossover is a bonus multiplier — next-door painters pour in after class every day, already energized from a creative experience. It's one of the most organic and loyal built-in audiences of any venue in Spokane.

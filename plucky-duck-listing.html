Spokane's Only Zero-Waste Store

The Plucky Duck is more than a making space — it's a mission. As Spokane's only zero-waste refillery, they sell eco-friendly household cleaners, personal care products, and sustainable goods alongside their DIY workshops. Customers who walk through the door are already self-selected for intentionality, community, and conscious living.

About This Opportunity

The Plucky Duck is Spokane's making space — a vibrant, family-friendly hub where people come to create, learn, and connect. They host hands-on workshops like soap making, bracelet making, journaling nights, and a growing menu of crafting experiences for all ages. They're also building out their workshop calendar, which means a growing and loyal audience of regulars who come back for each new event.

Workshop participants are engaged, present, and in a genuinely good mood — they've chosen to spend their free time making something with their hands. That's an unusually receptive moment for a brand to show up. A sample tucked into a materials kit, a card on the table, or a mention at the start of a workshop lands with warmth in a way that passive advertising simply can't replicate.

Beyond workshops, The Plucky Duck's zero-waste refillery draws eco-conscious Spokane shoppers who return regularly to refill — building a loyal repeat customer base that cares about supporting businesses aligned with their values.

About This Opportunity

Collins Dentistry & Aesthetics is a family-friendly dental practice with two locations — Spokane and Spokane Valley — led by Dr. Ken and Dr. Marnie Collins, who bring over 25 years of combined experience. What makes this placement valuable: dental visits are recurring (most patients return every 6 months for cleanings) and unhurried — patients sit in a comfortable waiting room before appointments, often with nothing to do but look around. That's a calm, captive moment for your brand to be seen by a loyal local patient base across two offices.

With an active Instagram and Facebook presence and a strong base of positive reviews, Collins Dentistry has built deep trust in the Spokane community — trust that extends to businesses they choose to feature.

📍 Dr. Mariella Zavala is an Assistant Professor of Marketing at Gonzaga University in Spokane, WA, and the founder of The Local Post Spokane — Spokane's local advertising marketplace. She holds a Ph.D. in Marketing from the University of California, Irvine (Paul Merage School of Business).

About Dr. Zavala

Dr. Mariella Zavala is an Assistant Professor of Marketing at Gonzaga University, where she teaches, mentors, and collaborates with students and communities to create meaningful, value-driven marketing. Her work is grounded in a simple belief: who you are and where you come from are strengths, not limitations.

In the classroom, she focuses on helping students connect theory to real-world marketing strategy — emphasizing ethical decision-making, critical thinking, and building confidence so students feel prepared to navigate a rapidly evolving landscape. Beyond teaching, she is passionate about making marketing more accessible: supporting small business owners, startups, organization leaders, and aspiring marketers in developing strategies that feel aligned with their identity, values, and goals.

Dr. Zavala is also the founder of The Local Post Spokane — a local advertising marketplace built to eliminate friction from local advertising and connect Spokane businesses, events, nonprofits, and organizations with the audiences that matter most to them.

Her research explores sustainability, marketing for social good, and decolonial perspectives in marketing. She also speaks about her personal journey navigating higher education and professional spaces with limited resources and great uncertainty — talks that center on building confidence and moving forward even when the next step isn't clear.

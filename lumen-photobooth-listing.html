About This Opportunity

Lumen Photobooth Co. is Spokane's modern luxury photo booth rental — founded by Tori, Spokane born and raised, to create elevated, effortless experiences at weddings, corporate events, birthday parties, school events, and celebrations of all kinds. Every booking is fully bespoke: custom photo templates, backdrop choices, a personalized booth interface, and a live gallery — all tailored specifically to the event.

From setup to breakdown, Tori handles everything. Guests just show up, pose, and walk away with a physical print and digital image they'll share and keep. The booth draws a crowd and a queue — and that queue is one of the warmest, most engaged advertising environments at any event. People waiting for their turn are fully present, in a great mood, and paying attention to everything around them.

Advertising here means your brand shows up at the most celebratory moments Spokane residents experience — not once, but at every Lumen event throughout the month, reaching couples, families, corporate teams, and community groups all across the area.

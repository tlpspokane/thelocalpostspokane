About This Opportunity

Black Straw Tea Bar & Kitchen is one of Spokane's most distinctive dining destinations — combining fresh, made-daily Chinese food with a full bubble tea menu in a cozy, welcoming atmosphere. With two locations (North Spokane at 6410 N Market St and Spokane Valley), they've built a loyal following of regulars who come back for lunch, dinner, and everything in between.

What makes this placement valuable is the rewards program and the repeat customer model. Black Straw diners are not one-time visitors — they earn points, track orders online, and return regularly to redeem. That's a digitally engaged, loyalty-oriented audience that's already in the habit of supporting a business they love.

Open 7 days a week with dine-in, takeout, and delivery options, Black Straw reaches a broad cross-section of Spokane and Spokane Valley residents across every meal occasion. Advertising here means consistent visibility with an audience that's already in a good mood — sitting down to food they chose on purpose.

About This Opportunity

TacoVado started as a drive-thru taco stand. After a few years building a loyal following, they opened a full restaurant on Northwest Blvd — and haven't looked back. The mission: give the community a space to hang out, make memories, and eat amazing food.

With a 4.8-star rating on DoorDash, 5,120 Instagram followers, and ordering available on Toast, DoorDash, GrubHub, and Uber Eats, TacoVado reaches customers across multiple channels. Their menu spans standard tacos and burritos, bowls, a full breakfast menu (served all day), and dedicated vegan, vegetarian, and keto sections — making this one of the most inclusive menus in Spokane.

"Now You Know" is printed on their merch and it says everything about the vibe: TacoVado is confident in their food and they want you to try it and decide for yourself. That energy — unpretentious, community-first, genuinely proud of their product — is exactly the kind of host that earns loyal customers and loyal advertisers.

About This Opportunity

Shelby's Burgers is Spokane's go-to for hand-smashed burgers, crispy fries, and boozy milkshakes — a lively counter-serve spot where everything is made to order and fresh every time. Their short rib and brisket blend, fried pickles, garlic rosemary parm fries, and signature boozy shakes have earned them a devoted following and consistently glowing 5-star reviews.

Open 7 days a week with online ordering via Toast and DoorDash, Shelby's reaches customers across multiple channels — dine-in, pickup, and delivery. They also cater events and private parties, and appear as a regular vendor at the Kendall Yards Night Market every Wednesday throughout the summer, bringing their audience to one of Spokane's most vibrant community gatherings.

Advertising here means your brand shows up in the middle of a genuinely good time — Shelby's customers are in celebratory mode. They're date nights, friend outings, and family treat nights. That's an audience already predisposed to notice and remember something good.

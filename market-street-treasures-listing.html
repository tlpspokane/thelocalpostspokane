About This Opportunity

Market Street Treasures is Hillyard's go-to spot for antiques, collectibles, jewelry, furniture, home décor, and vintage finds of all kinds. Owner Laura has built a welcoming, community-rooted store in the heart of the Market Street District — a neighborhood of local merchants that draws a loyal, return customer base of collectors, gift seekers, and treasure hunters.

What makes this listing unique in your portfolio is the multi-platform reach. Customers don't just find Market Street Treasures in person — they shop on eBay (store name: purposefulllybold), browse Facebook Marketplace, and watch live selling events on WhatNot. Advertising here means your brand can show up across all of those touchpoints, not just the physical storefront.

Inventory rotates constantly, which is why regulars come back often. The store buys and sells estate contents and collections, which brings in a diverse audience ranging from families clearing out a home to serious collectors hunting for a specific piece. There's also a seasonal flea market series in the Market Street District that draws a broader community crowd throughout the year.
